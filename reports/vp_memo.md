# Weekly Business Review: E-commerce Marketplace

**To:** Vice President  
**Subject:** Revenue growth, customer retention, delivery performance, and data reliability

## Executive Summary

The analysis of the Olist e-commerce dataset shows substantial revenue growth across much of 2017 and early 2018. However, repeat purchasing remains limited, delivery delays present an opportunity for improvement, and payment reconciliation issues need investigation before financial reporting can be considered fully reliable.

## Key Findings

1. **Revenue growth:** Delivered-item revenue reached 987,765.37 in November 2017, increasing 52.37% from October. Revenue then declined 26.50% in December 2017. These movements warrant further investigation into seasonality and order volume.

2. **Customer retention:** The repeat-purchase rate was 3.00%: 2,801 out of 93,358 unique customers placed at least two delivered orders. This provides a baseline for measuring future retention improvements.

3. **Delivery performance:** Of 96,470 eligible delivered orders, 6,534 were delivered late, giving a late-delivery rate of 6.77%. The analysis also found a higher late-delivery percentage among orders with low average review scores than among orders with high average review scores. This association does not establish causation.

4. **Data quality:** Payment reconciliation identified 260 mismatched orders among 98,665 comparable orders, a mismatch rate of 0.26%. Additional investigation identified 775 orders with payment records but no matching order items and one order with items but no payment record. These cases require investigation.

## Recommended Actions

1. Investigate the largest payment mismatches and document their causes and resolutions.
2. Review delivery delays by region and other available order characteristics to identify opportunities for improvement.
3. Develop customer-retention initiatives and track the repeat-purchase rate over time.
4. Run data-quality checks before publishing
